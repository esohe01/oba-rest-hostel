# Oba’s Rest Hotel — illustrative image prompts

These prompts are for representative AI-generated concepts, not photographs of Oba’s Rest Hotel. No images were generated in this stage because the image-generation tool is unavailable in this session. Save generated assets in this folder after generation, using the filenames below. Keep the visible “Illustrative image” labels when adding them to the website.

## Shared art direction

Use a consistent contemporary Nigerian boutique-hospitality visual style across all four images: believable architecture and materials, warm natural light, a restrained palette of warm ivory, soft sand, deep navy and muted brass, calm editorial composition, premium but realistic. Do not depict a specific real hotel or imply that any unconfirmed facility belongs to Oba’s Rest Hotel. No people, text, logos, signs, watermarks, exaggerated luxury or distorted architecture.

```text
Scene/backdrop: a plausible Benin City hospitality setting, with understated contemporary Nigerian design cues and realistic architectural materials.
Style/medium: editorial hospitality photography, natural texture, premium but believable.
Composition/framing: wide landscape frame, balanced architecture, clear visual focus, no reserved text area required.

Constraints: no identifiable real property, no hotel name, logo, text, sign, people, invented amenities, watermark or unrealistic luxury claim.

```
