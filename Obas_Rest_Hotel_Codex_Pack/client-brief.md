# Oba’s Rest Hotel — One-page client brief

**Client:** Oba’s Rest Hotel  
**Owner:** Mrs Eseosa Iyamu  
**Location:** Airport Road, Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria  
**Business:** Hotel and guest house accommodation

## The problem
Guests call at night to ask whether rooms are available, whether there is electricity and whether parking is available. These repeated enquiries interrupt the owner's rest and take time away from running the hotel.

## Current operation and confirmed facts
- Approximately **35 enquiries a week**, many at night.
- The hotel has **24-hour power**.
- **Parking is available for every room**.
- Events in Benin City can make the hotel fill up quickly.
- **Musa**, the receptionist, handles check-in.

## Non-negotiable business rule
**Never confirm a booking. Reception confirms every booking.**

The website chatbot may answer approved questions and direct guests to submit an enquiry. It must never claim that a room is available, reserved or confirmed. A request is not a reservation.

## Common customer questions
- Is there electricity/light?
- Is parking available?
- What time is check-in?
- What rooms do you have?
- Are rooms available for my dates?
- Where exactly is the hotel / how do I get directions?
- What amenities and house rules should I know about?
- What is nearby?

Some answers are not yet provided in the client card. The developer/AI assistant must ask the project owner for the missing information at the appropriate stage before adding it to the website or chatbot. Do not guess.

## Personal project scope
Build an individual, mobile-friendly website for Oba’s Rest Hotel, including:
- Home / hero section.
- Rooms section.
- Amenities section highlighting confirmed 24-hour power and parking for every room.
- Guest information / house rules section.
- Location and directions section.
- Booking-request / enquiry call-to-action.
- Embedded Chatbase chatbot using approved hotel information.

Create at least four consistent AI-generated illustrative images and retain the prompts used to generate them.

## Success looks like
Guests can find confirmed information quickly on a phone, ask common questions through the chatbot, and submit an enquiry without being misled into thinking a booking is confirmed. Reception remains responsible for confirming all bookings.
